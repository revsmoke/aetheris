# Aetheris User Guide

Welcome to Aetheris! This guide will help you navigate and use the platform to generate intelligent environmental reports for your architectural sites.

## Navigating the Interface
The Aetheris interface is designed to be immersive and clutter-free.
- **Dashboard (Bottom Right)**: Your main control center for search, metrics, and report generation.
- **3D Viewport**: Use your mouse to rotate and zoom around the 3D city tiles.

## Basic Workflow

### 1. Site Search
Enter a location or address in the **"Site Explorer"** search bar and hit Enter. The 3D scene will load the photorealistic tiles for that area, and the environmental sensors will begin measuring Solar and Air Quality metrics.

### 2. Visualizing Invisible Data
- The **Atmospheric Particles** you see floating in the scene represent real-time Air Quality data.
- **Green/Thin**: Good Air Quality.
- **Red/Dense**: High Pollution levels.

### 3. Placing Design Volumes
To test how a new building massing affects the environment:
1. Click **"Place Design Volume"**.
2. A representative architectural volume will appear in the center of the scene.
3. Use the **Time Slider** to change the time of day. Observe how the shadows cast by the volume move across the site in real-time.

### 4. Generating AIAI Reports
When you are ready for a deep analysis:
1. Click **"Generate AIAI Report"**.
2. Our **Architectural Intelligence AI (Gemini)** will analyze the current solar potential, air quality, and local pollen data.
3. A detailed design suggestion report will appear, recommending specific architectural interventions (like green roofs or solar baffles) tailored to that specific site.

## Tips for Architects
- Use the **Time Slider** to identify "dead zones" where shadows may negatively impact neighboring buildings.
- Look at the **Atmospheric Density** to determine where high-quality air filtration systems or "buffer lobbies" might be necessary for occupant comfort.
